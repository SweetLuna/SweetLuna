<summary><h2>🌙 Who I Am</h2></summary>
<br>

I've always felt a connection to **Greek** myth. **Asteria** scatters the stars, **Selene** carries the moon, and **Eos** paints the dawn. I took **Luna** for the moon, **Aurora** for the dawn, and the space between them is where my **heart** lives.

I build **Stable Diffusion checkpoints** like ***Aurora* and *Kenshi***, tuned for semi-realistic anime styles. I spend hundred of hours merging blocks by hand and baking LoRAs into the weights until the eyes finally look right. At the same year, I also became a verified Nexus Mods author (my SmoothCam preset crossed 200K downloads! Now 400k!) and I help out with **PROJECT Skyrim**. When I'm not merging models, I write small things in Node.js and JavaScript and exploring new things 💛

You can find me on Discord as `sweet_luna`, usually online somewhere past midnight.

<summary><h3>🔮 How the merges actually happen</h3></summary>
<br>

**Kenshi** started as two rough halves added together: an anime base (Shirayuki Anime V1) and a stronger anatomy model (protogenX3.4), combined into `ShiraProtogen`. From there it was five more weighted merges, each chasing a single fix: `NixeuCombine` for the base style, `NixeuGuweiz` for a semi-realistic look that broke the eyes, `NGBOW` to fix the eyes and lighting but rough up the hair, `NGBOWG` to bring the eyes back without losing the hair, `NGBOWGK` for brighter and more colorful eyes on zoom out, and a final blend back with `ShiraProtogen` to keep the anatomy honest.

**Aurora** took it further: `Kenshi` merged with AbyssOrangeMix2 and HoloKuki through a Merge Block Weighted pass I tuned by hand, block by block, so it would borrow AbyssOrangeMix2's anatomy without losing Kenshi's style. Eight LoRAs were baked permanently into the checkpoint afterward, and two negative embeddings, trained on roughly 200 of my own generations, ship alongside it along with an `SLE` trigger word just for the eyes.
